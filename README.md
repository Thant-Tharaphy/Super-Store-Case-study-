# Super-Store-Case-study-
Superstore business analytics using Python, Pandas, Seaborn and Matplotlib to explore sales, profit, customers, discounts, regions, trends and returns.
## Project Overview

This project analyses the Superstore retail dataset to evaluate overall business performance and identify the key factors affecting sales and profitability.

The analysis was completed in Python using Google Colab and focuses on products, customers, regions, discounts, shipping methods, customer segments, time-based performance, and product returns.

The main objective is to answer:

> How is the business performing, where is profit being generated or lost, and which areas require further investigation?

---

## Key Findings

The company generated approximately \$2.30 million in total sales and \$286,397 in profit, with an overall profit margin of 12.47%.

Technology was the strongest product category, while Furniture generated substantial revenue but had a much lower profit margin.

Phones generated the highest sub-category sales, while Copiers generated the highest profit. Tables were one of the major loss-making sub-categories.

The West was the strongest region for overall sales and profit. However, it also recorded the highest return rate at 11.73%.

Several high-sales locations, including Texas and Pennsylvania, generated negative profit, showing that strong revenue does not always translate into profitability.

The Consumer segment generated the highest total sales and profit, while Home Office achieved the highest profit margin.

Discount analysis showed that transactions with discounts of 30% and above were associated with negative profitability.

Sales and profit generally increased over the four-year period, with 2018 recording the highest annual sales and profit. However, some months experienced increasing sales while profit decreased.

The overall order return rate was 5.91%. Machines had the highest sub-category return rate at 11.61%, followed by Tables at 9.77%.

---

## Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the company should investigate:

- High discount levels, particularly discounts of 30% or more
- Loss-making sub-categories such as Tables
- High-sales but unprofitable customers
- Poor-performing states and cities
- The unusually high return rate in the West region
- High-return sub-categories such as Machines and Tables

The company should focus not only on increasing sales but also on protecting profit margins and reducing unnecessary returns and discounting.
